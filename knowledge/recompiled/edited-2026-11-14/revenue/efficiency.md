## Strike Selection Patterns

Strikes 11–22% OTM have produced consistent disciplined wins; select strike distance by the OTM buffer tolerable if spot touches the top of the expected range. See [lesson:short_call.strike_and_sizing].

## Buyback Patterns

A buyback is insurance, not a rescue: once most of the premium has decayed, buying back below strike is a disciplined exit when its certain cost is smaller than the risk it removes, and spot later staying below strike does not make it a mistake. Assess strike distance, DTE, IV trend, and momentum before any buyback. See [lesson:short_call.exit_insurance] and [lesson:process.decision_quality].
